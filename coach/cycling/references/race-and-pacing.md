# Race Pacing Strategy & Fueling Guidelines

Quantitative pacing formulas, gradient-based power allocation rules, and intra-event nutrition strategies for endurance cycling events.

---

## 1. Pacing Targets by Event Duration & Format

Pacing accuracy is the single greatest determinant of endurance performance. Prescribe Target Intensity Factor (IF) based on duration:

| Event Type / Duration | Target IF (% FTP) | Target VI | Primary Pacing Risk |
| ----------------------- | ------------------- | ----------- | --------------------- |
| **10k / 20k Time Trial ($< 30\text{ mins}$)** | $102\% - 105\%$ | $< 1.03$ | Starting too fast in first 3 minutes |
| **40k Time Trial ($~60\text{ mins}$)** | $98\% - 102\%$ | $< 1.03$ | Over-powering headwind segments |
| **Gran Fondo / Century ($3 - 5\text{ hours}$)** | $75\% - 82\%$ | $< 1.08$ | Surging on early climbs ($> 110\%$) |
| **Ultra-Endurance ($> 6\text{ hours}$)** | $65\% - 73\%$ | $< 1.05$ | Depleting glycogen, under-fueling |

---

## 2. Gradient-Based Pacing Rules

Air resistance ($F_d \propto v^2$) makes surging on flat roads inefficient, whereas climbing gravity ($F_g \propto m \cdot g \cdot \sin\theta$) rewards higher power allocation on steep gradients.

```
                    ┌────────────────────────────┐
                    │    Gradient Pacing Rule    │
                    └─────────────┬──────────────┘
                                  │
    ┌─────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┐
    ▼                             ▼                             ▼
Flat / Downhill              Modest Climbs                 Steep Climbs
 (< 2% Gradient)              (3% - 6% Gradient)            (> 7% Gradient)
    │                             │                             │
    ▼                             ▼                             ▼
Target: 90-95% Target IF      Target: 100-105% Target IF    Target: 105-110% Target IF
 • Aerodynamic tuck            • Steady cadence              • Cap power at 115% FTP
 • Never surge                 • Seated power                • Avoid standing surges
```

### Climbing Power Caps

- **Long Climbs ($> 20\text{ minutes}$)**: Cap max power at $105\% \text{ FTP}$. Average power should stay at $93-98\% \text{ FTP}$.
- **Short Climbs ($2 - 5\text{ minutes}$)**: Cap max power at $120\% \text{ FTP}$. Ensure W' reserve is not depleted.

## 3. Carbohydrate & Hydration Strategy

Fueling rate dictates performance preservation past the 90-minute mark.

```
                       ┌────────────────────────────────┐
                       │ Hourly Fueling & Fluid Targets │
                       └───────────────┬────────────────┘
                                       │
       ┌───────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┐
       ▼                               ▼                               ▼
Standard Duration               High Intensity / Long            Hot Conditions
 ($1.5 - 3.0\text{ hours}$)      ($> 3.0\text{ hours}$)           ($> 28^\circ\text{C} / 82^\circ\text{F}$)
       │                               │                               │
       ▼                               ▼                               ▼
60 - 75g Carbs / hr             90 - 120g Carbs / hr            750 - 1000ml Fluid / hr
 • Single transport carb         • 2:1 Glucose : Fructose        • 700 - 1000mg Sodium / L
   (Glucose/Maltodextrin)          hydrogel or drink mix          • Pre-hydrate with sodium
```

### Carbo-Loading Taper Protocol ($48\text{ Hours}$ Pre-Race)

- **Carbohydrate Target**: $8 - 10\text{ g/kg}$ body weight per 24 hours.
- **Dietary Adjustments**: Low fiber, low fat, high glycemic carbohydrates (white rice, pasta, bagels, fruit juice) to maximize muscle glycogen storage without GI distress.

---

## 4. Pre-Race Checklist & Taper Execution

1. **7 Days Out**: Final key workout (3x4m VO2max touch). Reduce volume by $40\%$.
2. **3 Days Out**: Short openers session ($45\text{ mins}$ with 3x1m @ Race Pace).
3. **1 Day Out**: Activation spin ($30\text{ mins}$ Z1 with 2x15s sprints).
4. **Race Morning**: Breakfast $3\text{ hours}$ prior ($2 - 3\text{ g/kg}$ carb). Hydrate with $500\text{ml}$ fluid + electrolytes.
