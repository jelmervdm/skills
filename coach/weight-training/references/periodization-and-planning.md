# Weight Training Periodization & Concurrent Training

Mesocycle structures for Hypertrophy, Strength, Power, and concurrent endurance integration.

---

## 1. Resistance Mesocycle Models

- **Hypertrophy Accumulation (4-6 Weeks)**: 8–15 reps per set @ 1–3 RIR. Focus on volume load progression ($10-20\text{ sets/muscle group/week}$).
- **Strength Intensification (3-4 Weeks)**: 3–6 reps per set @ 1–2 RIR ($\ge 80\% \text{ 1RM}$). Focus on maximal motor unit recruitment.
- **Peaking / Realization (1-2 Weeks)**: 1–3 reps @ 1–2 RIR ($\ge 90\% \text{ 1RM}$). Volume drop by $50\%$.

---
