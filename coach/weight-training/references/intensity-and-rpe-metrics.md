# Weight Training Intensity, RPE / RIR Metrics & VBT

Comprehensive guide for Reps in Reserve (RIR), RPE for resistance training, percentage of 1RM conversion, Velocity-Based Training (VBT), and Volume Load.

---

## 1. Reps in Reserve (RIR) & RPE Chart

| RPE Scale | Reps in Reserve (RIR) | Exertion & Proximity to Failure |
| ----------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **10** | **0 RIR** | Maximal effort. No more reps could be completed. |
| **9.5** | **0-1 RIR** | Could not do more reps, but could take slightly more load. |
| **9** | **1 RIR** | 1 rep definitely left in the tank. |
| **8.5** | **1-2 RIR** | 1-2 reps left in the tank. |
| **8** | **2 RIR** | 2 reps left in the tank (Optimal strength & hypertrophy balance). |
| **7.5** | **2-3 RIR** | Fast bar speed, 2-3 reps left. |
| **7** | **3 RIR** | Heavy warm-up or speed work pace (3 reps left). |
| **6** | **4 RIR** | Light warm-up pace. |

---

## 2. Percentage 1RM to Rep RIR Matrix

$$\text{Estimated 1RM (E1RM)} = \text{Weight} \times \left(1 + \frac{\text{Reps}}{30}\right)$$

*(or using Wathan / Epley 1RM conversions).*

---

## 3. Volume Load & Velocity-Based Training (VBT)

- **Volume Load**:
  $$\text{Volume Load} = \text{Sets} \times \text{Reps} \times \text{Weight (kg)}$$
- **Velocity Loss (% Intraset Velocity Decay)**:
  - **Hypertrophy Target**: $20\% - 40\%$ velocity loss from first to last rep.
  - **Maximal Strength / Power Target**: $10\% - 20\%$ velocity loss (terminate set before bar speed slows significantly).
