# Weight Training Workout Analysis & Performance Tracking

Structured evaluation of completed strength sessions, RIR accuracy diagnostics, rep performance decay, and progressive overload tracking.

---

## 1. RIR Accuracy & Calibration

Swapping weight mid-set or over-shooting target RIR indicates calibration error:
- **Under-shooting (Target 2 RIR, Actual 4 RIR)**: Insufficient mechanical tension for maximal muscle fiber recruitment. Increase weight $2.5 - 5\%$.
- **Over-shooting (Target 2 RIR, Actual 0 RIR / Failure)**: Excessive fatigue accumulation. Reduce weight $2.5 - 5\%$ for subsequent sets.

---

## 2. Progressive Overload Framework

Progressive overload is achieved across mesocycles via:
1. **Load Progression**: Increasing weight while maintaining target reps & RIR.
2. **Rep Progression**: Adding reps at the same weight (e.g. 8 reps -> 10 reps @ 2 RIR).
3. **Volume Set Progression**: Adding 1 set per muscle group per week (e.g. 12 sets/wk -> 14 sets/wk).
