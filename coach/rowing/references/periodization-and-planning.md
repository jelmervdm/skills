# Rowing Periodization & Training Planning

Macrocycle structuring, seasonal training blocks, set notation, and volume distribution for indoor ergometer racing and on-water crew preparation.

---

## 1. Annual Training Macrocycle

Rowing periodization follows a classic polarized model with high volume at low stroke rates during winter, transitioning to high-rate threshold and race-specific intervals in spring.

```
[ Autumn Head Race Phase ] ---> [ Winter Base (UT2/UT1) ] ---> [ Spring 2K Prep (AT/TR) ] ---> [ Peak 2K Taper ]
       (Sept - Nov)                     (Dec - Feb)                    (March - May)                   (June)
```

---

## 2. Seasonal Training Blocks

### Autumn 6K / Head Race Block (Volume & Threshold)

- **Focus**: Aerobic capacity, 6K split endurance, 24–28 SPM rate caps.
- **Key Workout**: $3 \times 15\text{ min @ AT pace (2K + 7s) / 3m rest}$.

### Winter Ergometer Base Block (High Yardage UT2)

- **Focus**: Polarized endurance ($75–80\%$ UT2 volume), low SPM (18–20), structural core strength.
- **Key Workout**: $2 \times 30\text{ min @ UT2 pace (2K + 18s) @ 18 SPM / 4m rest}$.

### Spring 2K Sprint Block (VO2max & Speed)

- **Focus**: 2K race pace precision, 30–34 SPM cadence, lactate clearance.
- **Key Workout**: $4 \times 1000\text{m @ 2K pace / 5m rest}$ or $8 \times 500\text{m @ 2K - 2s / 3m rest}$.

---

## 3. Standard Rowing Set Notation

Prescribe workouts using clear split, rate, and rest parameters:

- `2 x 30m UT2 @ 19 SPM (2K+18s) w/ 3m rest`
- `4 x 1000m @ 28 SPM (Target 1:45.0) w/ 4m rest`
- `8 x 500m @ 32 SPM (2K-2s) w/ 3m rest`
- `Pyramid: 250m-500m-750m-1000m-750m-500m-250m @ 2K Pace w/ 1:1 Rest`
