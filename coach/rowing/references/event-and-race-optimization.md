# Rowing Event & Race Optimization

Pacing strategies, mental preparation, start protocols, and tactical execution for 2K ergometer sprint tests, 5K/6K head races, and crew racing.

---

## 1. 2K Ergometer Race Execution Strategy

A 2K ergometer race is a 6 to 8-minute effort that requires precise split distribution to prevent premature glycogen depletion while maximizing average wattage.

```
[ 0 - 250m ]     Start Sprint (High SPM 36-40, settle by stroke 15)
[ 250 - 1000m ]  Settle Phase (Target 2K Split + 0.5s @ 32 SPM)
[ 1000 - 1500m ] Third 500m Move (Focus leg drive, maintain split)
[ 1500 - 2000m ] Final Sprint (Build SPM 34-38+, All-Out drive)
```

### 2K Split Breakdown Guide

- **First 250m**: 5 high strokes + 10 building strokes ($2-3\text{s}$ faster than target split). Settle onto target split by stroke 15.
- **250m – 1000m**: Settle into target split rhythm at target stroke rate ($30–34\text{ SPM}$).
- **1000m – 1500m ("The Wall")**: Execute a planned 10-stroke burst ("10 for leg drive") at 1000m to prevent split decay.
- **1500m – 2000m (Redline)**: With 500m to go, increase SPM by $+2$ every 150m and kick final 250m all-out.

---

## 2. 5K / 6K Autumn Head Race Pacing

- **Target Split**: $\text{2K Split} + 6\text{ to }8\text{ seconds}$.
- **Stroke Rate**: $26–28\text{ SPM}$.
- **Tactics**:
  - Establish rhythm early in the first 500m.
  - Call 10-stroke moves every 1000m or on bridge/turn exits to maintain boat momentum.
  - Maintain wide steering turns on water to minimize drag and velocity loss.

---

## 3. Taper & Pre-Race Protocols

- **7-Day Race Taper**: Reduce overall training volume by $40–50\%$ while maintaining short race-pace bursts ($4 \times 250\text{m @ 2K Pace}$).
- **Warmup Protocol (30 min prior to race)**:
  - 10 min easy rowing (UT2).
  - $3 \times 10$ stroke bursts building to race SPM.
  - 5 min rest before entering race start or setting ergometer monitor.
