# Ultra-Volume Readiness & Blood Chemistry Monitoring

Guidelines for preventing non-functional overreaching, monitoring serum ferritin/iron, and post-Ironman recovery.

---

## 1. Systemic Markers & Ferritin Awareness

Ultra-distance volume ($12-20\text{ hours/week}$) strains red blood cell synthesis and immune health:

- **Serum Ferritin Check**: Athletes with low ferritin ($< 35\text{ ng/ml}$) experience chronic fatigue and low aerobic capacity.
- **Post-Ironman Recovery**: Mandate 14 days of zero structured intensity and minimal volume following a 140.6 race.
