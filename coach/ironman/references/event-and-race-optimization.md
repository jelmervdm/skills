# Ironman Race Day Tactics & Special Needs Setup

Special needs bag configuration, aid station mechanics, and mental association techniques for 140.6 races.

---

## 1. Special Needs Bag Configuration

- **Bike Special Needs (Km 90)**: Reserve solid food option (e.g. chews, small PB&J sandwich), extra salt capsules, spare inner tube, back-up sunscreen.
- **Run Special Needs (Km 21.1)**: Back-up gels, blister pads/bandages, fresh pair of dry socks, anti-chafing cream, mental motivation card.

---

## 2. Aid Station Mechanics

- **Bike Aid Stations**: Slow down to $15\text{km/h}$, call out bottle request ("WATER!"), grab bottle, discard empty bottle in trash zone.
- **Run Aid Stations**: Walk through every aid station (30 seconds of walking prevents overheating and ensures complete fluid ingestion).
