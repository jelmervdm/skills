# Ironman Pacing Budgets & Metabolic Targets (70.3 & 140.6)

Comprehensive reference for target Intensity Factor (IF), Variability Index ($\text{VI}$), and run pace targets for 70.3 and 140.6 events.

---

## 1. Pacing Target Matrix

| Event Distance | Swim Target | Bike Intensity Factor (IF) | Bike VI Limit | Marathon / Half-Marathon Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **70.3 (Half)** | CSS $+ 3 - 5\text{s / 100m}$ | IF $0.75 - 0.80$ ($75-80\% \text{ FTP}$) | $\text{VI} < 1.05$ | Open Half-Marathon Pace $+ 15 - 25\text{s/km}$ |
| **140.6 (Full)** | CSS $+ 6 - 10\text{s / 100m}$ | IF $0.68 - 0.73$ ($68-73\% \text{ FTP}$) | $\text{VI} < 1.04$ | Open Marathon Pace $+ 30 - 45\text{s/km}$ |

---

## 2. Fat Oxidation & Glycogen Preservation

- **Fatmax Zone ($60-70\% \text{ FTP}$)**: Primary energy source for 140.6. Maximizes fat oxidation ($\sim 0.8-1.0\text{g fat/min}$) while preserving liver and muscle glycogen.
- **Over-Biking Penalty**: Exceeding target IF by 5% doubles glycogen burn rate ($\text{CHO}$ oxidation rate surges exponentially above $75\% \text{ FTP}$).
