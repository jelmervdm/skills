# Multisport Load Balancing & Composite Stress Tracking

Framework for tracking cumulative multi-sport stress ($\text{TSS} + \text{rTSS} + \text{sTSS}$) and balancing training hours across Swimming, Cycling, and Running.

---

## 1. Composite Training Stress Score (cTSS)

In multi-sport training, acute fatigue is systemic:

$$\text{cTSS} = \text{TSS}_{\text{Bike}} + \text{rTSS}_{\text{Run}} + \text{sTSS}_{\text{Swim}}$$

- **Target Weekly cTSS Ranges**:
  - **Sprint Distance Athlete**: $300 - 450 \text{ cTSS/week}$
  - **Olympic Distance Athlete**: $450 - 650 \text{ cTSS/week}$

---

## 2. Weekly Time Allocation Matrix

| Discipline | Volume Share | Key Session Focus |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Swim** | $15\% - 20\%$ | 2-3 sessions/week (Focus: Hydrodynamics & CSS Threshold) |
| **Bike** | $50\% - 55\%$ | 2-3 sessions/week (Focus: Aerobic Base & Sweet Spot / Threshold) |
| **Run** | $25\% - 30\%$ | 3 sessions/week (Focus: Threshold Tempo, Easy, Brick Run) |
