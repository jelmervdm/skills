# Triathlon Readiness & Recovery Management

Guidelines for managing multi-discipline recovery, joint strain prevention, and systemic fatigue detection.

---

## 1. Systemic vs Discipline-Specific Fatigue Diagnostics

- **Cardiovascular Strain (Elevated RHR, Low HRV)**: Reduce intensity across all 3 disciplines; substitute easy Z1 swim/spin.
- **Achilles / Soleus Strain (Post-Brick Run)**: Cancel running for 3–5 days. Substitute extra Z2 cycling or pool swimming to maintain cardiovascular fitness without impact force.
- **Shoulder Impingement (Post-Swim)**: Reduce swim volume by $50\%$; eliminate hand paddles.
