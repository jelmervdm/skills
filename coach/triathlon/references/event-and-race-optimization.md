# Triathlon Event Optimization & Race Pacing

Sprint and Olympic distance race pacing budgets, transition setup layout, and race-day nutrition strategies.

---

## 1. Pacing Budget Matrix

| Distance | Swim Pace | Bike Intensity Factor (IF) | Run Pace Target |
|----------|-----------|----------------------------|-----------------|
| **Sprint** | CSS $- 2\text{s / 100m}$ | IF $0.90 - 0.95$ ($90-95\% \text{ FTP}$) | 5k Open Race Pace |
| **Olympic** | CSS Pace | IF $0.85 - 0.88$ ($85-88\% \text{ FTP}$) | 10k Open Race Pace $+ 5 - 10\text{s/km}$ |

---

## 2. Race Nutrition & Hydration

- **Sprint**: 1 gel on bike + $500\text{ml}$ fluid.
- **Olympic**: $60\text{g}$ carbohydrate per hour on bike ($500-750\text{ml}$ fluid/hr) + 1 gel at T2 transition start.
