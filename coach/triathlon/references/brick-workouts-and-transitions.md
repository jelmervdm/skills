# Brick Workouts & Transition Mechanics (T1 & T2)

Detailed guide for bike-to-run brick workouts, T1 (Swim-to-Bike) execution, and T2 (Bike-to-Run) rapid setup.

---

## 1. Brick Workout Protocols

Brick workouts train the central nervous system to adapt rapidly from cycling kinematics (fixed hip flexor angle, high cadence) to running stride dynamics.

- **Standard T2 Brick**: 60-90m Bike @ Target Race IF + 15-20m Run off the bike.
- **Micro-Brick Sets**: 3x (15m Bike @ Threshold + 1km Run @ Race Pace) w/ rapid T2 swaps.

---

## 2. Transition Mechanics (T1 & T2)

- **T1 (Swim to Bike)**: Strip cap/goggles on run to bike rack. Helmet ON before touching bike. Elastic speed laces or pre-clipped bike shoes.
- **T2 (Bike to Run)**: Rack bike before unbuckling helmet. Swap to running shoes with elastic laces. Grab race belt & gel.
- **Target Times**: T1 $< 2\text{ minutes}$, T2 $< 1\text{ minute}$.
