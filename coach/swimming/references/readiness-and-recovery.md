# Swim Shoulder Health & Readiness Management

Guidelines for preventing shoulder impingement, managing rotator cuff strain, and regulating swim volume.

---

## 1. Shoulder Impingement & Rotator Cuff Red Flags

Due to thousands of arm revolutions per workout, swimmers are highly prone to supraspinatus and biceps tendon strain.

- **Impingement Sign (Pain at $90^\circ-120^\circ$ abduction)**: Immediately remove hand paddles and butterfly stroke. Switch to smooth freestyle or kickboard work.
- **Paddle Restriction**: Cap hand paddle usage at $\le 30\%$ of weekly volume. Never use oversized paddles if shoulder tightness is present.
- **Dryland Prehab Requirement**: Mandate 2x/week rotator cuff internal/external rotation drills with resistance bands (Y-T-W drills).
