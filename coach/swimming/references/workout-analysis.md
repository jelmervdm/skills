# Swim Workout Analysis & Hydrodynamic Efficiency

Diagnostic framework for evaluating swim efficiency, SWOLF scores, stroke rate vs. distance per stroke, and pacing decay.

---

## 1. SWOLF Score & Efficiency

SWOLF measures combined time and stroke count for a pool length:

$$\text{SWOLF} = \text{Time per length (sec)} + \text{Stroke Count per length}$$

- **Lower SWOLF = Higher Hydrodynamic Efficiency**.
- **Example (25m Pool)**: 20 seconds + 15 strokes = SWOLF 35.

---

## 2. Distance Per Stroke (DPS) vs. Stroke Rate (SR)

- **Distance Per Stroke (DPS)**: Meters traveled per arm cycle. Higher DPS indicates clean catch and minimal drag.
- **Stroke Rate (SR)**: Cycles per minute (spm).
- **Fatigue Breakdown Rule**: As lats and shoulders fatigue, swimmers tend to increase SR while DPS drops precipitously, resulting in high turbulence and slower times.
