# Running Periodization & Plan Construction

Principles of distance running periodization, weekly mileage ramp rules, and training block templates.

---

## 1. Weekly Mileage Progression (The 10% Rule)

- **Maximum Ramp Rate**: Never increase weekly distance by more than $10\%$ over the preceding 3-week average.
- **Step-Down Week**: Every 3–4 weeks, reduce total weekly mileage by $25-30\%$ to allow bone and connective tissue remodeling.

---

## 2. Distance Periodization Blocks

### 5k / 10k Training Block (12 Weeks)

- **Base (Weeks 1-4)**: Easy volume building, striders (R-Pace touches).
- **Support (Weeks 5-8)**: Threshold tempo runs ($T\text{-Pace}$) + Hill repeats.
- **Peak (Weeks 9-11)**: VO2max Intervals ($I\text{-Pace}$, e.g., 5x1000m w/ 3m rest).
- **Taper (Week 12)**: Volume $-50\%$, 3x400m race pace openers.

### Marathon Training Block (16 Weeks)

- **Base (Weeks 1-6)**: Long easy runs building to $22-25\text{km}$.
- **Threshold & M-Pace (Weeks 7-12)**: Long runs incorporating 10-15km at Marathon Pace ($M\text{-Pace}$).
- **Peak Long Run (Weeks 13-14)**: 30-32km long run (3 weeks out).
- **Taper (Weeks 15-16)**: 2-week exponential taper (Volume $-30\%$, then $-50\%$).
