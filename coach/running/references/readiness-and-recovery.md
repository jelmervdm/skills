# Running Impact Stress & Recovery Management

Protocol for evaluating running impact fatigue, monitoring bone/tendon health, and preventing overuse injuries.

---

## 1. Musculoskeletal Red Flag System

Unlike non-impact sports (cycling/swimming), running subjects legs to $2.5 - 3.0\times$ body weight in ground reaction force per foot strike.

| Pain Character | Diagnostic Suspicion | Immediate Action |
|----------------|----------------------|------------------|
| **Dull Aches (Fades with warmup)** | Muscle tightness / DOMS | Proceed with Easy (E) run; foam roll. |
| **Point Tenderness on Bone (Shin, Metatarsal)** | Stress Fracture / Reaction | STOP RUNNING IMMEDIATELY. Cross-train (swim/bike) 7-14 days. |
| **Sharp Tendon Pain (Achilles, Patellar)** | Tendonitis / Tendinopathy | Cancel high-speed/hill work; load eccentrically (calf raises). |
| **Unilateral GCT Asymmetry ($> 3\%$)** | Compensatory Guarding | Reduce volume by $50\%$. Inspect shoes for wear ($> 600\text{km}$). |

---

## 2. Recovery Protocols & Surface Selection

- **Surface Rotation**: Alternate concrete/asphalt with soft surfaces (dirt trails, grass, synthetic track) to decrease peak bone impact forces.
- **Shoe Retirement**: Replace running shoes every $500 - 800\text{km}$ ($300 - 500\text{ miles}$) as foam midsole damping degrades.
