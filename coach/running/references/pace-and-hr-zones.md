# Running Pace & HR Zones (Jack Daniels VDOT & Metrics)

Comprehensive reference for Jack Daniels VDOT formula, physiological running zones, Grade-Adjusted Pace (GAP), running power, and running training stress (rTSS).

---

## 1. Jack Daniels VDOT Training Zones

VDOT represents an athlete's effective VO2max based on recent race performances, accounting for running economy.

| Zone | Name | % of Max HR | Description & Purpose |
| ------ | ------ | ------------- | ----------------------- |
| **E** | Easy / Recovery | $65\% - 78\%$ | Aerobic foundation, capillary building, active recovery |
| **M** | Marathon Pace | $80\% - 85\%$ | Marathon specific pace, glycogen storage adaptation |
| **T** | Threshold Pace | $88\% - 92\%$ | Lactate clearance threshold, comfortably hard ($~60\text{m}$ max pace) |
| **I** | Interval (VO2max) | $95\% - 100\%$ | Maximal oxygen uptake, $3 - 5\text{m}$ interval reps |
| **R** | Repetition | N/A (Sprint) | Anaerobic speed, running economy, neuromuscular turnover ($200-400\text{m}$) |

## 2. Grade-Adjusted Pace (GAP) & Running Power

### Grade-Adjusted Pace (GAP)

Adjusts pace on uphill and downhill terrain to estimate equivalent flat-ground exertion:

- **Uphill**: Pace slows mechanically, but GAP reflects the higher metabolic effort.
- **Downhill**: Pace speeds up, but steep downhills ($> 8\%$) increase muscle damage (eccentric strain).

### Running Power (Watts) & rTSS

- **Running Power**: Measures total mechanical work rate (Stryd/Garmin). Less susceptible to environmental lag than HR.
- **rTSS (Running Training Stress Score)**:
  $$\text{rTSS} = \frac{\text{Duration (seconds)} \times \text{NGP} \times \text{IF}}{\text{TP} \times 3600} \times 100$$
  *(where NGP = Normalized Graded Pace, TP = Threshold Pace)*.
