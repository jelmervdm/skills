# XC Skiing Event Optimization & Marathon Race Tactics

Tactical protocols for Vasaloppet (90km), Birkebeiner (54km), and high-altitude ski marathon events.

---

## 1. Ski Marathon Pacing & Drafting Tactics

- **Drafting Line**: Skiing behind a lead skier saves up to $15-20\%$ aerodynamic drag on flat lakes/open valleys.
- **Pure Double-Poling (DP) Strategy**: In modern ski marathons (e.g. Vasaloppet), elite racers use pure double poling on un-waxed skate/slick classic skis. Requires immense core and lat endurance.
- **Warm Fueling Protocol**: Consume warm electrolyte drink with carbohydrate ($60-80\text{g/hr}$) from insulated drink belts.
