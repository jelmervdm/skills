# XC Skiing Respiratory Health & Overuse Diagnostics

Guidelines for managing cold-air airway safety, triceps/elbow tendonitis, and lower back strain.

---

## 1. Exercise-Induced Bronchoconstriction (Cold Air Airway Safety)

Breathing high volumes ($> 100\text{ L/min}$) of cold, dry air ($< -10^\circ\text{C}$) causes airway dehydration and severe bronchospasm:
- **Thermal Heat-Exchange Mask (Airtrim)**: Mandatory for hard interval sessions when temperature drops below $-12^\circ\text{C}$ ($10^\circ\text{F}$).
- **Temperature Cut-Off**: Cancel high-intensity interval workouts below $-18^\circ\text{C}$ ($0^\circ\text{F}$).

---

## 2. Roller-Ski Impact & Elbow Protection

Roller-skiing on asphalt transmits sharp impact forces through poles into elbows:
- **Ferrule Care**: Use sharp carbide tips; avoid hard pavement where possible.
- **Triceps Tendonitis**: At first sign of elbow tendon pain, replace double-poling workouts with leg-only skate sessions.
