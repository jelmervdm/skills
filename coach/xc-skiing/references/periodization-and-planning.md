# XC Skiing Periodization & Seasonal Planning

Year-round periodization across Off-Season Dryland, Pre-Snow Autumn, and Winter On-Snow racing.

---

## 1. Seasonal Phase Blueprint

- **General Preparation (May - August)**: Roller skiing (Z2), trail running, hill bounding with poles, heavy upper-body strength (pullups, dip bars, core).
- **Specific Preparation (September - November)**: High-intensity roller ski intervals (V2 & Double-Pole threshold sets), SkiErg intervals.
- **On-Snow Base & Race Phase (December - March)**: On-snow volume, subtechnique refinement, ski marathon races (Vasaloppet, Birkebeiner, Engadin).
- **Transition / Recovery (April)**: Low-volume cross-training, active rest.
