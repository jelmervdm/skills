# XC Skiing Workout Analysis & Kinematic Diagnostics

Framework for analyzing completed ski/roller-ski workouts, poling cycle length, and power distribution.

---

## 1. Poling Kinematics & Cycle Length

- **Cycle Length (Meters per stroke)**: Higher cycle length indicates efficient weight transfer and glide extension.
- **Poling Cadence (Cycles/min)**:
  - **V2 / Double Pole**: $45 - 60 \text{ cycles/min}$
  - **V1 Steep Climb**: $60 - 75 \text{ cycles/min}$
- **Upper-Body vs Lower-Body Load Split**: Double-poling places up to $60\%$ of total propulsion force onto upper-body muscles (latissimus dorsi, rectus abdominis, triceps).
