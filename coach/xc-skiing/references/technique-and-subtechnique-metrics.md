# XC Skiing Subtechnique Kinematics & Metrics

Comprehensive reference for Skate (Freestyle) and Classic subtechniques, gear ratios, and terrain matching.

---

## 1. Skate (Freestyle) Subtechniques

| Subtechnique | Common Name | Optimal Terrain Gradient | Poling & Leg Timing |
|--------------|-------------|--------------------------|---------------------|
| **V1** | Offset | Steep Climbs ($> 6 - 8\%$) | 1 pole plant for every 2 skate pushes (Asymmetric poling). |
| **V2** | One-Dance | Modest Climbs & Flats ($2 - 5\%$) | 1 pole plant for EVERY single skate push (Symmetric poling). |
| **V2-Alternate** | Two-Dance | Fast Flats & Downhills ($0 - 2\%$) | 1 pole plant for every 2 skate pushes (Poling on alternate sides). |
| **Open Field Skate** | Free Skate | High-Speed Downhills | Leg push only; poles tucked under armpits. |

---

## 2. Classic Subtechniques

| Subtechnique | Optimal Terrain | Mechanics & Focus |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Diagonal Stride** | Moderate Climbs | Alternating arm/leg drive; requires grip wax/skin kick zone engagement. |
| **Double Poling (DP)** | Flats & Gentle Grades | Simultaneous symmetrical poling; zero leg kick. High core flex. |
| **Kick Double Pole** | Flat to Low Incline | Single leg stride followed by double pole plant. |
| **Herringbone** | Very Steep Climbs | Skis angled out in V-shape; walk up steep pitches without slipping back. |
